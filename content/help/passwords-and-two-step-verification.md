Use a long password that you use nowhere else, and turn on two-step verification. If you forget your password you can reset it by email. If you lose your phone you can sign in with a recovery code.

## Choosing a password

Use at least 10 characters. A few random words strung together is both strong and easy to remember. We reject very common passwords and ones that contain your email name or handle. Use a password you use nowhere else, and consider a password manager.

## Forgot your password

Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email address. If it belongs to an account, we send a link that works once and expires after one hour. Using it signs you out everywhere. For safety, the page looks the same whether or not the address has an account.

## Changing your password

Open [Settings](/settings/security), enter your current password and then the new one. Changing it signs out your other devices.

## Turn on two-step verification (2FA)

Two-step verification, also called 2FA, asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app each time you sign in, as well as your password. It stops anyone who has only your password.

1. Open [Settings](/settings/security) and choose **Set up two-step verification**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Aegis, 2FAS or your password manager. If you cannot scan it, type the key shown below it.
3. Type the 6-digit code to confirm. You then get **recovery codes**. Save them somewhere safe.

Each code from the app works once. Each recovery code also works once.

## Lost your phone

Sign in with your password and one of your recovery codes instead of the app code. Then turn two-step verification off and set it up again on your new phone. If you have no recovery codes left, write to us from the [contact page](/contact). We will need to check that you own the account, so it can take a while.

## See where you are signed in

[Settings](/settings/security) lists your active sessions, with the browser and a rough location. Sign out any you do not recognise, and change your password.

## If you think someone else got into your account

Do these in order: change your password in [Settings](/settings/security) (this signs out every other device), turn on two-step verification, then look at the list of places you are signed in and sign out any you do not recognise. If you cannot sign in, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. If something was posted or changed that you did not do, write to us from the [contact page](/contact) and choose **Account or sign-in problem**.
