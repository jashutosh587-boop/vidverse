Videos are ranked by how much of them people actually watch, not by how many click. A misleading title or thumbnail does not help a video. No one can pay to rank higher.

## What goes into the ranking

Home, Up next and search are ordered by a quality score. It mixes five things:

- **How much of the video people actually watch.** This counts the most, and it is squared, so a video that many people start and quickly leave falls down the page. That is what usually happens with a misleading title or thumbnail.
- **Likes compared with dislikes.** Counted gently, so one early like or dislike does not decide anything.
- **How many people watched recently.** On a gentle scale, so a huge channel does not drown everyone else.
- **How new it is.** Newer videos get a boost that fades over several weeks.
- **A fair start.** A brand-new video with few views gets a short extra boost, so its first viewers can tell us whether it is any good.

Subscriber counts, and how often a title uses capital letters or exclamation marks, are not part of the score.

## For you

If you are signed in, videos in categories and with tags like the ones you watched recently get a small lift. Videos you already finished are shown less. No one can pay to rank higher. To start fresh, clear your history on the [History page](/history).

## Variety

No single channel can fill the top of a list. After a couple of videos from the same channel, the rest move further down.

## Trending

Trending counts viewers over the last three days and weighs them by how much of each video they watched.

## Search

Search first matches your words against titles, tags and descriptions, and then orders matches using the same viewer-quality signal. Channel matches are shown above the videos.
