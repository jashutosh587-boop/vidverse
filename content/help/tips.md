{{#payments}}
A tip is a voluntary gift that a viewer sends to a creator. The creator receives it minus a platform fee, once a short holding period has passed.

## Sending a tip

Press **Support** on a channel or a video. You need an account with a confirmed email address, so the creator and we can deal with problems. Choose an amount between {{tipMin}} and {{tipMax}}, add a short message if you like (up to 140 characters), and choose whether the creator sees your name or sees **Anonymous**. You then pay on a secure page run by our payment provider, using the methods it offers, such as UPI, cards and net banking. We never see or store your card or bank details.

A tip is a gift. It does not buy anything, and it does not change how a video is ranked.

## What the creator receives

The creator receives the tip minus a {{feePercent}}% platform fee, which covers payment-processing charges and running costs. For example, on a tip of ₹100 the creator receives {{exampleNet}} and {{exampleFee}} is the fee. The creator sees both numbers.

The money appears in the creator's balance only after the payment provider confirms the payment to us. If you were charged but do not see a confirmation after a few minutes, write to us from the [contact page](/contact) and keep your payment reference.

## The holding period

Each tip is held for {{holdDays}} days before the creator can withdraw it. The delay gives time to deal with refunds, disputes and mistakes.

## Refunds

Tips are voluntary gifts and are generally not refundable. We will help if you were charged twice, if someone else used your payment method, or if the law requires a refund. Write to us from the [contact page](/contact) as soon as you notice. If a tip is refunded or reversed, it is taken out of the creator's balance, including from later earnings if it has already been paid out.

## For creators

You can see every tip, its fee and its status on your Earnings page. You cannot tip your own channel.
