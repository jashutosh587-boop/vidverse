Honest titles and thumbnails bring the right viewers and help a video last. Misleading ones lose viewers within seconds and lower where the video appears.

## Titles

A good title says what the video is. It can be catchy, but the video has to deliver what it promises. Up to 100 characters. Capital letters and exclamation marks do not rank anything higher here.

## Thumbnails

Choose one of the three frames we take from your video, or upload your own image: JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 3 MB, 16:9 and at least 1280 by 720. A thumbnail should show something that is in the video. If yours looks blurry, start from a sharper image: small images are rejected, and photos of a screen or heavily compressed pictures look soft once they are enlarged.

## Why misleading titles and thumbnails backfire

Viewers who click and find something different leave quickly. We rank videos by how much of them people actually watch, so a misleading title or thumbnail lowers where your video appears, for you and for every video after it. Viewers can also report a **misleading title or thumbnail**, and repeated misleading videos can be removed.

## Descriptions and timestamps

The description can be up to 5,000 characters. Write a time such as `1:25` or `12:03` and viewers can click it to jump to that moment. Links in descriptions open in a new tab.

## Tags and categories

Pick the category that fits best. Add up to ten tags that describe the video. They help the right people find it in search. Adding unrelated tags does not help and can get a video removed.
