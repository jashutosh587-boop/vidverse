Earning here is meant for real people supporting real work. These things are not allowed:

- **Tipping yourself,** directly or through accounts or payment methods you control, to move money around or to get a payout.
- **Buying or trading views, likes, comments or subscribers,** or using bots, scripts, click farms or "engagement groups".
- **Paying people to watch,** or tricking people into clicking, for example with a misleading title or thumbnail.
- **Using stolen cards or payment methods,** or disputing a tip you made.
- **Giving false identity or payout details.**

## What happens

If we find any of these, we may remove the views, withhold or reverse the money involved, hold payouts while we look into it, remove videos, and suspend or close the account. Money that was paid for a tip that is later reversed is taken from the creator's balance, including from later earnings. Serious or repeated cases are reported to the payment provider and, where the law requires, to the authorities.

## If you think we got it wrong

Write to us from the [contact page](/contact). A person will look at the facts and reply.
