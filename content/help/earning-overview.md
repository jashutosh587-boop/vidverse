Creators can earn from tips that viewers send, from support links on their own channel, and from deals they make themselves. Ad income from this site is not shared with creators.

## The ways to earn

{{#payments}}
1. **Tips from viewers.** Viewers can send you a tip from your channel or from one of your videos. We keep {{feePercent}}% as a platform fee and you keep the rest. See [Tips](/help/tips).
{{/payments}}
{{^payments}}
1. **Tips from viewers.** Tips are not switched on at the moment on this site.
{{/payments}}
2. **Your own support links.** You can add links to your channel, such as a UPI link, a membership page or a shop. Viewers use them directly with those services. We do not process these payments and take no cut.
3. **Sponsors and brand deals.** You can make your own deals. If a brand paid you or gave you something for a video, tick **includes paid promotion** when you upload, so viewers see a label. We take no share of these deals.
4. **Shared revenue, if we offer it.** If we ever share other income with creators, such as money from a sponsor or a membership programme we run, it is divided by watch time and announced here. Nothing of this kind is paid out unless it shows up on your Earnings page.

## What is not offered

Advertising income from the ads on {{siteName}} is not shared with creators. See [Ads and AdSense](/help/ads-and-adsense) for why.

## An honest expectation

Most channels earn little or nothing at first. Tips depend on viewers choosing to send them, and viewers do that for channels they care about. Nothing here guarantees income, and we do not promise any amount. Consistent, honest videos that people watch to the end are what grow a channel.

## Where to see your money

Open **Earnings** from your account menu. It shows your balance, what is still being held, tips received and payout requests. See [Payouts](/help/payouts).

## Rules

Earning is for real viewers and real support. See [Earning rules](/help/earning-rules) and the [creator terms](/legal/creator-terms).
