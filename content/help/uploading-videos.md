To publish a video, open Studio and choose Upload. We accept MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and AVI files, convert them for smooth streaming, and tell you when the video is ready.

## Before you start

You need an account with a confirmed email address. The first time you open Studio you will be asked to read the [creator terms](/legal/creator-terms) and the [community guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines). Only upload videos you have the right to share.

## File types and limits

- **Types:** MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and AVI.
- **Size:** up to {{maxUpload}} per video.
- **Length:** up to {{maxDuration}}.
- **Per day:** you can start up to {{uploadsPerDay}} uploads a day.
- **Total storage:** {{storageQuota}} across all your videos. Delete a video you no longer need to make room.

## Publishing, step by step

1. Open **Studio**, then **Upload**.
2. Choose your file. Fill in a title (up to 100 characters), a description, tags (up to ten), and a category.
3. Choose who can watch (see below) and whether comments are on.
4. If a brand paid you or gave you something for the video, tick **includes paid promotion**. A label is shown on the video.
5. Wait for the upload bar to finish. Keep the page open. If the upload is interrupted you start it again from the beginning.

## What processing does, and if it is stuck

After the upload, we convert your video into several qualities (up to 1080p, never above your original), so it plays smoothly on slow and fast connections, and we pick three frames to choose a thumbnail from. This takes a few minutes for a short video and longer for a long one. You get a notification when it is ready. Only you can see the video until then. If a video stays on *Processing* much longer than you expect, reload the page. If it has not finished after an hour, write to us from the [contact page](/contact) with the video's title.

If processing fails, the reason is shown in Studio. Common causes are a damaged file, a file with no picture, or a video longer than the limit. Exporting again as MP4 usually fixes the rest.

## Who can watch

- **Public:** anyone can find it on Home, in search and on your channel.
- **Unlisted:** only people with the link can watch. It is not shown in search or on your channel, and search engines are asked to skip it. Anyone with the link can share it.
- **Private:** only you.

You can change this at any time from the video's page in Studio.

## Changing a video after you publish it

Open the video in Studio and choose **Edit**. You can change the title, description, tags, category, who can watch, whether comments are on, the paid-promotion label and the thumbnail at any time. You cannot replace the video file or cut and trim it here. To change the picture or sound, upload a new video.

## Deleting a video

Open the video in Studio and choose **Delete**. The video files, its comments, likes and history entries are removed for good. Tips already paid for the video stay in your earnings.
