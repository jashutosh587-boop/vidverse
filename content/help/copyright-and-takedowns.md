If someone uploaded your work without permission, send us a copyright notice from the contact page. If your own video was removed by mistake, you can send a counter-notice.

## If someone uses your work

Write to us from the [contact page](/contact) and choose the topic **Copyright**. Include:

1. Your full name, the rights holder you represent, and an email address.
2. What the original work is, and where we can see it.
3. The address of each video on {{siteName}} that uses it.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not allowed by you, your agent or the law.
5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the rights holder or are allowed to act for them.
6. Your typed full name as a signature.

Think first about whether the use might be allowed, for example a short quotation for review or criticism. A notice that is false or careless can have legal consequences for the sender.

## What we do

We check the notice. If it is complete and the use looks unauthorised, we remove the video or block access to it, and tell the uploader, including the notice and your contact details. If a notice is incomplete, we ask for what is missing.

## If your video was removed

You are told the reason. If you believe it was a mistake, or that you have permission or a right to use the material, write to us from the [contact page](/contact) with the topic **Copyright** and include: the address of the removed video, a statement that you believe it was removed by mistake or misidentification, why, your name and contact details, and your typed name as a signature. We pass your reply to the person who complained. Unless they show us, within the time the law allows, that they have started court action or have a court order, we may restore the video.

## Repeat infringement

Accounts that repeatedly upload other people's work without permission are suspended or closed. See the [copyright policy](/legal/copyright).
