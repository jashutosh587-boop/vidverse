You may be unable to sign in because of a wrong password, a temporary lock after too many attempts, an unconfirmed email address, or a suspension.

## "Those details do not match"

The sign-in page shows the same message whether the email address is wrong, the password is wrong, or the account is locked. This is deliberate: it stops other people finding out which email addresses have accounts. Check the address and password, or [reset your password](/forgot-password).

## Temporary lock

After five wrong passwords or codes in a row, the account is locked for one minute. Each further failure doubles the wait, up to one hour. A correct password and code after the lock ends works normally. Resetting your password clears the lock straight away.

## You have not confirmed your email address

You cannot sign in until you open the link in the confirmation email. The link works once and lasts 24 hours. On the sign-in page, choose **Send the confirmation email again**. Check your spam folder if nothing arrives.

## Suspended accounts

A moderator can suspend an account that breaks the [community guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) or the [Terms](/legal/terms). A suspended account is signed out everywhere, its videos are hidden, and the sign-in page shows the reason. You can still write to us from the [contact page](/contact) to ask for a review. Say who you are and why you think the decision was a mistake, and we will look at it again.
