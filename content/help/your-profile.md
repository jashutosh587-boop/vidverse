Change your display name, description, avatar colour and support links in Settings. Your handle and email address cannot be changed by you.

## What other people see

Your channel shows your display name, your handle, your description, your avatar, the number of subscribers and your public videos. The About tab shows when you joined and any support links you added. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

## Changing your profile

Open [Settings](/settings/profile). You can change:

- **Display name,** up to 50 characters.
- **Description,** up to 1,000 characters.
- **Avatar colour.** Your avatar is a colour with your first initial. Uploading a picture is not available yet.
- **Support links,** up to four, each with a short label. Only web addresses starting with https:// or http:// are accepted, and they open in a new tab. We do not check where they lead and we do not take part in payments made through them, so only follow links you trust.

## Handle

Your handle is part of your channel address (`/@handle`) and cannot be changed after you sign up. If you need a different one, write to us from the [contact page](/contact).

## Email address

Changing the email address on an account is not available yet. If you need to, write to us from the [contact page](/contact) from the old address.
