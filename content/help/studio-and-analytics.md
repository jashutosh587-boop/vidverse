Studio shows how your videos are doing: views, watch time, subscribers and how much of each video people watch. A view counts after 30 seconds of watching.

## Your dashboard

Studio shows the last 28 days: views, total watch time, your subscribers and how many are new. It also lists your most-watched videos. Each video has its own page with a chart of views per day and the share of the video people watched on average.

## What counts as a view

A view counts once someone has watched 30 seconds of a video, or half of it if the video is shorter than a minute. Each viewer counts at most once a day for the same video. Your own watching does not count, and neither does traffic we recognise as automated. That is why our numbers can be lower than on other sites.

## Average viewed

This is the average share of the video that viewers watched, for example 55%. It is the number that matters most for reach, because it is the biggest part of the ranking. See [How recommendations work](/help/how-recommendations-work).

## Watch time

The total time people spent watching your videos. The player reports time in small steps while a video plays. A single report can claim at most 30 seconds and never more than the real time that has passed, so skipping around or leaving a tab open does not inflate it.

## Delays

Numbers can lag by a few minutes.

## Milestones

You get a notification when a video reaches 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000 and 1,000,000 views.
