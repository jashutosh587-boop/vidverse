Ads help pay for the site. Creators are not paid a share of ad income, because Google's AdSense rules do not allow it without Google's written permission. Creators earn from tips and their own support links instead.

## Where ads appear

{{#ads}}
We show ads from Google AdSense on video pages, next to public videos that have finished processing. We do not show ads on private or unlisted videos, in the player, on sign-in or settings pages, or to the owner of a video while they look at their own video. A video that has been reported is shown without ads until a moderator has looked at it.
{{/ads}}
{{^ads}}
This site is not showing ads at the moment. If that changes, ads will appear only next to public videos on video pages, never inside the player and never on sign-in, settings or Studio pages.
{{/ads}}

## Who earns from ads, and why creators are not paid from them

Ads help pay the people who run {{siteName}} and the biggest costs of any video site, which are storage and streaming. Creators cannot get paid from ad income: Google's AdSense programme policies do not allow a site to pass AdSense earnings on to other people unless Google has agreed in writing. Unless we say otherwise on your Earnings page, ad income is not shared with creators. That is why creator income here comes from tips and from your own support links instead. See [Earning money as a creator](/help/earning-overview).

{{#ads}}
## Your choices

{{#personalisedAds}}
Ads may be personalised. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads are used only if you agree in the consent message you are shown. You can change your choice, or turn personalised ads off, in your Google ad settings.
{{/personalisedAds}}
{{^personalisedAds}}
We ask Google to show **non-personalised** ads. They are chosen from the page you are on, not from your browsing history or your account. {{siteName}} does not give Google your name, email or watch history.
{{/personalisedAds}}

An ad blocker works fine here. Ads are never needed to watch.
{{/ads}}

## If an ad is a problem

Tell us from the [contact page](/contact) with a screenshot and the address of the page, and we will pass it on to Google and look at the video it appeared on.
