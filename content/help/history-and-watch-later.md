While you are signed in, {{siteName}} keeps your watch history, your Watch later list and your liked videos. Only you can see them, and you can remove them at any time.

## Watch history

While you are signed in, each video you watch is added to your [history](/history), with how far you got. History powers **Continue watching** and gives your recommendations a small nudge. Only you can see it.

- **Remove one video:** use *Remove from history* under it.
- **Clear everything:** use *Clear all watch history* at the bottom of the page. This also empties Continue watching and resets what your recommendations learned from you.

When you are not signed in, we do not keep a history for you.

## Watch later

Press **Watch later** under a video to keep it on your [Watch later](/watch-later) list. Remove it from there when you have watched it, or no longer want it.

## Liked videos

The thumbs-up saves a video to your [liked videos](/liked), which only you can see. Likes and dislikes are counted on the video, and the video's owner sees a total, not who voted.
