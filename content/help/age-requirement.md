You must be 18 or older to create an account. You confirm this when you sign up. Accounts allow commenting, uploading and receiving tips, and we are not set up to do those safely for children.

## Children

Please do not create an account if you are under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and remove its data.

## Parents and guardians

If your child has an account, or you have a concern about their safety here, write to us from the [contact page](/contact). We will close the account. Videos here are not made for children, and we cannot guarantee that every video suits them. We cannot tell the age of someone who watches without signing in.

## Reporting a child-safety concern

Use **Report** and choose **Child safety**. Those reports are looked at first. See [Reporting content](/help/reporting-content).
