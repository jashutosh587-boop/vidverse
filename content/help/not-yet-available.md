This is a plain list of things that are **not** available on {{siteName}} at the moment. It is here so you do not spend time looking for them. It is not a promise that any of them will arrive, or when.

## For viewers

- **Mobile apps.** There is no app to install. The website works in a mobile browser.
- **Downloading videos to watch offline.** Videos can only be watched on the site.
- **Pausing your watch history.** You can remove single videos or clear all of it from the [History page](/history), but you cannot switch it off.
- **Editing a comment after posting.** You can delete a comment and write it again.

## For creators

- **Live streaming.** The site only has uploaded videos.
- **Captions and subtitles.** You cannot add a caption or subtitle file to a video.
- **Playlists.** You can save videos to Watch later, but you cannot make playlists.
- **Scheduling a video to publish later.** A video is public, unlisted or private as soon as it has finished processing. You can keep it private until you are ready.
- **Your own profile picture.** Your avatar is a colour with your initial. You choose the colour in [Settings](/settings/profile).
- **Verified badges.**

## For your account

- **Changing your email address.** There is no setting for it yet. Write to us from the [contact page](/contact) and we will look into it after checking that the account is yours.

If there is something you need that is not on this list, tell us from the [contact page](/contact) and choose **Feedback**.
