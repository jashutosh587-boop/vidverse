The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) is the full legal text. This page is the short version.

## What we keep

- **Your account:** name, handle, email address, a scrambled version of your password (we cannot read it), your settings, and when you agreed to the terms.
- **What you do here:** videos you upload, comments, likes, subscriptions, saved videos, and, while you are signed in, your watch history.
- **For security:** when and from which browser you signed in (the network address is shortened), failed attempts, and actions by moderators and admins.
- **For payments:** tips you send or receive, payout requests and payout details. Card and bank details for tips are handled by the payment provider, not by us.

When you are not signed in we count a view without storing who you are. A scrambled identifier that changes every day is used so one person is not counted twice.

## Do you sell my data?

No. We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the services that run this site for us, such as hosting, email and payment providers, with people the law requires us to tell, and with a creator when you tip them under your own name.
{{#ads}}Google, which serves the ads you may see here, receives some information from your browser when an ad loads. See [Ads and AdSense](/help/ads-and-adsense).
{{/ads}}
## How it is protected

Passwords are stored with a slow one-way scheme. Payout details and the secret for two-step verification are encrypted. Sessions can be ended from Settings. Pages are served over HTTPS and send strict security headers. No system is perfectly safe. If something we hold about you is exposed, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Controls

- **Download your data:** [Settings, Privacy](/settings/privacy) gives you a file with your account, videos' details, comments, history and money records.
- **Edit it:** change your profile in [Settings](/settings/profile).
- **Watch history:** remove videos or clear all of it on the [History page](/history).
- **Delete your account:** see [Deleting your account](/help/deleting-your-account).
- **Ads:** see [Ads and AdSense](/help/ads-and-adsense).

## Who sees what

Your watch history, likes, saved videos and time watched are private. Your comments, channel and public videos are public. A tip's sender is shown to the creator unless the tip was marked anonymous.
