These are the main rules. The [community guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) have the full text and examples, and apply to videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, comments and profiles.

## Not allowed

- Sexual content involving anyone under 18, or any sexualising of minors. We report this to the authorities.
- Pornography and sexually explicit material.
- Threats, incitement to violence, and content that praises or helps violent groups.
- Harassment, bullying, doxxing (sharing private details) and hate against people for who they are.
- Content that encourages self-harm, eating disorders or dangerous challenges.
- Scams, fake giveaways, phishing and links to malware.
- Spam and fake engagement, including buying or trading views, likes or subscribers.
- Pretending to be someone else, or misleading people about what a video is.
- Other people's work without permission. See [Copyright and takedowns](/help/copyright-and-takedowns).
- Anything illegal in India.

## Allowed, with care

News, education, art and documentary material can include difficult subjects when it informs rather than glorifies, and when it is clearly explained. We may add a warning or limit who can find it.

## What happens

Depending on how serious the break is, we may remove the content, limit ads on it, suspend an account, or close it. We aim to explain the reason and give a way to ask for a second look.
