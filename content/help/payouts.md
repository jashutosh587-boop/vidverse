Creators can withdraw their earnings once the available balance reaches the minimum payout of {{payoutMin}}. You add a UPI ID or bank account, request a payout, and a person reviews it and pays it.

## How your balance works

Your balance is the total of everything that has been credited to you minus everything you have already withdrawn. It has two parts:

- **Available:** money that has passed its {{holdDays}}-day holding period. This is what you can withdraw.
- **Pending:** recent money still in the holding period.

Both are on your **Earnings** page. Every entry there is permanent: we never edit or delete past entries. Corrections are added as new entries, so the history always adds up.

## Adding payout details

On the **Payouts** page, add a UPI ID or bank account (account holder name, account number and IFSC code). It should be in your own name. We store the details encrypted, show you only the last few characters, and use them only to pay you.

## Requesting a payout

The minimum payout is {{payoutMin}}. You can request a payout once your available balance reaches it. Choose an amount up to your available balance. The amount leaves your balance immediately, so you cannot spend it twice. You can have one open request at a time, and you can cancel it while it is still waiting, which puts the amount back.

## What happens next, and when you are paid

A person reviews each request, then pays it to your UPI ID or bank account and records the payment reference. Requests are handled in the order they arrive. We cannot promise a date, but the status on your Payouts page tells you where yours is. You get a notification when it is paid or if it is declined. If it is declined, the amount returns to your balance and the reason is shown. We may ask you to confirm your identity or details before paying.

## Tax

You are responsible for the tax on what you earn. Where the law requires us to collect tax details, such as a PAN, or to withhold tax, we will tell you before we do. Payouts may be delayed until we have what the law requires.

## If your account is closed or suspended

While we investigate a serious rule break, we may hold payouts. You cannot delete your account while you have a balance or an open payout request. Withdraw it first, or write to us if it is below the minimum.
