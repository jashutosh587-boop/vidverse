People with an account and a confirmed email address can comment and reply. You can delete your own comments, and the owner of a video can remove any comment on it.

## Writing a comment

You need an account with a confirmed email address to comment. A comment can be up to 1,000 characters long and have at most two links. You can post up to 20 an hour, and posting the same comment on the same video twice within ten minutes is blocked. These limits keep spam down.

## Replying

Choose **Reply** under a comment. Replies go one level deep. The person you reply to, and the owner of the video, are notified.

## Removing a comment

You can delete your own comments. The owner of a video can remove any comment on it, and moderators can remove comments that break the [community guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines). Removing a comment also removes its replies.

## Turning comments off

When you upload or edit a video you can switch comments off for that video. Existing comments are hidden while comments are off.

## Reporting a comment

Use **Report** next to a comment. See [Reporting content](/help/reporting-content).
