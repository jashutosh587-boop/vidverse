The player adapts to your connection, remembers the speed you like, and picks up where you stopped. It also has keyboard shortcuts.

## Quality and speed

Under the video you can change **Quality** and **Speed**. Quality is *Auto* by default: the player picks the best quality your connection can keep up with and changes it if your connection changes. Choose a fixed quality to save data or to stop it switching. Speed runs from 0.5x to 2x, and the player remembers your choice on your device.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| K | Play or pause |
| J | Back 10 seconds |
| L | Forward 10 seconds |
| M | Mute or unmute |
| F | Full screen |

## Pick up where you stopped

When you are signed in, we remember where you stopped in each video. Open it again, or use **Continue watching** on the home page, and it carries on from there. Times in a video's description, such as 1:25, are links that jump to that moment.

## If a video will not play

- A video you uploaded is only visible to you until processing has finished. See [Uploading videos](/help/uploading-videos).
- Check your connection and try a lower quality.
- Reload the page, or try another current browser.
- Still stuck? Write to us from the [contact page](/contact) with the video's address and the browser you use.
