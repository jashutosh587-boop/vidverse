Use the [contact page](/contact) to write to us. A person reads every message.

## What to include

Say what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the address of the page if you have it. If you are signed in, we attach your account to the message, so you do not need to repeat your email address. Never send your password, a one-time code or a full card number. We will never ask for them.

## Which page to use

- **Copyright:** if someone is using your work without permission, follow [Copyright and takedowns](/help/copyright-and-takedowns).
- **Something harmful in a video, comment or channel:** use the **Report** link next to it. See [Reporting content](/help/reporting-content). Reports go to our moderators, who look at child-safety reports first.
- **A complaint about how we handled something, or a privacy request:** write to the [grievance officer](/legal/grievance).
- **Anything else:** the contact page.

## What to expect

We reply by email, to the address you give us. Most replies take a few working days. The help assistant cannot see your account, so it cannot answer questions about your own videos, balance or payouts. Those need a person.
