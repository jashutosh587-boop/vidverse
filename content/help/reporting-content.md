Use the Report link under a video or next to a comment. Moderators review reports, with child-safety reports first. You need to be signed in to report.

## How to report

Press **Report** under a video or next to a comment, choose the reason that fits best, and add any detail that helps us. You need to be signed in. To report a channel, report one of its videos or comments, or write to us from the [contact page](/contact). You can report the same thing only once while a report is open.

Reasons you can choose: spam or scam, misleading title or thumbnail, harassment or bullying, hate speech, violent or dangerous content, sexual content, child safety, copyright, illegal content, or something else. For copyright, use [Copyright and takedowns](/help/copyright-and-takedowns) instead, because we need more details.

## What happens next

Reports go to our moderators. Reports about child safety are looked at first. A moderator can dismiss the report, remove the video or comment, or suspend the account. A video with an open report is shown without ads until it has been checked. We do not tell the person you reported that it was you.

We cannot discuss the outcome of a report in detail. If a video is removed, the uploader is told the reason.

## In an emergency

If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency services first. In India the emergency number is 112. If a child is at risk, call Childline on 1098. You can also report child sexual abuse material and online crime to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in. Reporting it here as well helps us remove it, but it is not a replacement.

## If we got it wrong

If your video or comment was removed and you think that was a mistake, write to us from the [contact page](/contact).
