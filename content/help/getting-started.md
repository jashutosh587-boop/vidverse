{{siteName}} is a video site. Anyone can watch. People with an account can follow channels, comment, keep a watch history, upload their own videos and, if they want, receive tips from viewers.

## Watching without an account

You can watch public videos, search and open channels without signing in. Your place in a video, your history and your likes are only kept when you are signed in.

## Creating an account

1. Choose **Create account** at the top of any page.
2. Enter your name, a handle (your channel address will be `/@handle`), your email address and a password of at least 10 characters.
3. Confirm that you are 18 or older and that you agree to the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) after reading the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).
4. Open the email we send and click the link in it. The link works once and expires after 24 hours. You cannot sign in until you have done this.

A few random words make a strong password. More in [Passwords and two-step verification](/help/passwords-and-two-step-verification).

## Finding videos

- **Home** shows videos we think are worth your time. See [How recommendations work](/help/how-recommendations-work).
- **Trending** shows what many people watched over the last few days.
- **Search** looks at titles, tags and descriptions, and at channel names.
- **Subscriptions** lists new videos from channels you follow.

## Where things are

The menu on the left (the menu button at the top on a phone) links to Home, Trending, Subscriptions, History, Watch later and Liked videos. Your account menu at the top right has your channel, Studio, Earnings, Settings and Help. The **Help** button at the bottom right opens the help assistant.

## Want to publish your own videos?

Open **Studio** from your account menu. You will be asked to read the creator terms once, and then you can upload. See [Uploading videos](/help/uploading-videos).
