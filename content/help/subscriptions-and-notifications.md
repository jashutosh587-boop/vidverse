Subscribing follows a channel. Notifications are opt-in: press the bell next to Subscribe to be told when that channel publishes.

## Subscribing

Press **Subscribe** on a video or a channel page. Your **Subscriptions** page lists the newest videos from those channels, newest first. You can unsubscribe at any time from the same button. You cannot subscribe to your own channel.

## The bell

Subscribing does **not** notify you by itself. To get a notification when a channel publishes, press the bell next to the Subscribe button. Press it again to turn it off.

## What else can notify you

- A comment on one of your videos, and a reply to your comment.
- Your video finishing processing, or failing.
- A tip arriving, and updates on payout requests.
- Milestones for your videos, such as the first 100 views.
- A notice from us about the rules, if one is needed.

Notifications appear under the bell at the top of the page. There are no push notifications and no marketing emails. You can read them all at once from the [notifications page](/notifications).
