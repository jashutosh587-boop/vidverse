You can delete your account yourself from Settings. It happens straight away and cannot be undone. It is blocked while you have money in your balance.

## How to delete

Open [Settings, Privacy](/settings/privacy), choose **Delete my account**, type your password and the word shown, and confirm. It happens straight away and **cannot be undone**. You may want to download your data first from the same page.

Your channel belongs to your account, so a channel cannot be deleted on its own. To remove a single video instead, open it in Studio and choose **Delete**.

## If it will not let you

You cannot delete your account while you have money in your balance (available or pending) or an open payout request. Request a payout and wait for it to be paid. If your balance is below the minimum payout, write to us from the [contact page](/contact) and we will sort it out.

## What is removed

- Your profile, channel, bio and links. Your name and handle stop appearing anywhere.
- Your email address and password.
- Every video you uploaded, with its files, comments, likes and view history.
- Your comments elsewhere, your likes, subscriptions, saved videos, watch history, notifications and open sessions.

## What we keep

We keep a minimal record that an account existed, without your name or email, together with the records the law requires us to keep, such as payments and payouts, and the log of actions by moderators. Copies in backups are removed as the backups are replaced on their normal schedule.

## If your account was suspended

Deleting an account does not clear a suspension for rule breaks. Write to us if you want to appeal.
