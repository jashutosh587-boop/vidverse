We want you to come back because something here was worth your time, not because the site is built to hold on to you. These are the controls that keep you in charge.

## Autoplay

When a video ends, the next one in *Up next* can start after a five-second countdown that you can cancel. The **Autoplay** switch under the video turns this off. When you are signed in, the choice is saved to your account; otherwise it is saved in your browser. After three videos in a row that started on their own, we stop and ask **Still watching?** and wait for your answer.

## Daily reminder

In [Settings](/settings/preferences) you can pick a daily time between 10 minutes and 24 hours. Once you have watched that much in a day, a note appears at the top of every page. It never locks you out. It only tells you.

## See how much you watch

[History](/history) shows how long you watched today and your daily average over the last 7 days, with a small chart. Only you can see it. You can remove single videos from your history or clear all of it.

## What we do not do

- We do not send "come back" or "we miss you" emails. We email you to confirm your address, to reset a password, and to answer messages you send us.
- Notifications are opt-in. You only get them for channels where you turned on the bell, plus comments and replies on your own videos.
- Lists use pages, not endless scrolling.
- Videos are ranked by how much of them people actually watch, not by how many click. See [How recommendations work](/help/how-recommendations-work).
