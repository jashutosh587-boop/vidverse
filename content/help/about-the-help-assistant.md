The **Help** button at the bottom right opens an assistant that answers questions about how {{siteName}} works.

## How it answers

It searches our help articles for the passages that best match your question and shows the most relevant one, with links to the full articles. {{#aiAssistant}}An AI language model may reword that passage to fit your question. It is shown only the matching help passages and your question, never anything from your account, and it is told to answer only from those passages.{{/aiAssistant}}{{^aiAssistant}}It does not use an AI language model at the moment: the answer is the article text itself.{{/aiAssistant}} If the articles do not cover your question, it says so instead of guessing.

## What it cannot do

- It cannot see your account, videos, balance or payouts, and it cannot change anything. For those, use the [contact page](/contact).
- It does not give legal, tax or financial advice. Read the [policies](/legal) or ask a professional.
- It can be wrong or out of date. The article it links to is the reference.

## Your questions

We do not save the questions you ask the assistant, and we do not link them to your account. They are not used for ads or recommendations.{{#aiAssistant}} When the AI model is used, your question and the matching help passages are sent to our AI provider to produce the reply.{{/aiAssistant}}

Questions in English work best.
