{{siteName}} respects the rights of creators and expects its users to do the same. This policy applies from {{effectiveDate}}.

## 1. Our rule

Upload only what you made or have permission to use, including the music, pictures and clips in it. Using someone else's work without permission can break the law and this policy. We remove infringing content and close the accounts of repeat infringers.

## 2. Notice to us

If you believe a video here infringes your copyright or another right you hold, write to us from the [contact page](/contact) and choose the topic **Copyright**, or email {{contactEmail}}. Your notice must include:

1. Your full name, the rights holder you represent, your address and an email address where we can reach you.
2. A description of the work you say is infringed, and where we can see the original.
3. The address (URL) of each video you want us to act on.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and that you are the rights holder or are authorised to act for them.
6. Your typed full name as a signature.

Sending a false or careless notice can lead to legal consequences for you. Think about whether the use might be permitted, for example as a short quotation for criticism or review.

## 3. What we do

We check each notice. If it is complete and the use appears unauthorised, we remove the video or block access to it, and tell the uploader and give them the notice, including your contact details. We may ask you for more information. We may act sooner where a video plainly copies a work.

## 4. Counter-notice

If your video was removed and you believe the removal was a mistake, or that you have permission or a legal right to use the material, write to us from the [contact page](/contact) with the topic **Copyright**. Include the address of the removed video, a statement that you believe it was removed by mistake or by misidentification and the reason, your name, address and email, and your typed name as a signature. We pass your reply to the person who complained. Unless they show us, within the time the law allows, that they have begun court action or have obtained an order, we may restore the video.

## 5. Repeat infringers

We suspend or close the accounts of people who repeatedly upload infringing material.

## 6. Other rights

The same process applies to trademark and other intellectual property complaints. For anything else, such as privacy or defamation, use the [grievance officer](/legal/grievance).

## 7. Reports that are not about copyright

Use **Report** next to the video or comment for anything that breaks the [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines).
