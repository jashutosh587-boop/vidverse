## How we handle complaints

1. We aim to acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours.
2. We aim to resolve it within 15 days. If we cannot, we tell you why and when you can expect an answer.
3. We tell you what we decided and why.

Please include your name, the email address of your account, what happened, the address of any page involved, and what you would like us to do. Do not send your password.

## Other routes

- To report a video or comment that breaks the rules, use **Report** next to it. See [Reporting content](/help/reporting-content).
- For copyright, see the [Copyright policy](/legal/copyright).
- For ordinary questions and account problems, use the [contact page](/contact).

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may also approach the authority or court that the law allows for your complaint.
