This page applies from {{effectiveDate}}. It explains what {{siteName}} stores in your browser, and how ads work when they are shown.

## Cookies we set

Every cookie below is needed to run the site securely. None is for tracking or advertising.

| Name | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `__Host-vv_sid` | Keeps you signed in. Set only when you sign in. Not readable by page scripts. | Up to 60 days, and ends after 14 days of inactivity |
| `__Host-vv_csrf` | A random value that lets us tell your forms apart from forged ones. Set for every visitor. | About 13 months |
| `__Host-vv_flash` | Carries a one-time message, such as "Video deleted", to the next page. | 2 minutes |

## Browser storage we use

These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

| Name | What it stores |
|---|---|
| `vv-theme` | Whether you chose the light or dark look |
| `vv-rail` | Whether you collapsed the side menu |
| `vv-speed` | Your chosen playback speed |
| `vv-autoplay` | Your autoplay choice, if you are not signed in |
| `vv-autoplay-chain`, `vv-autoplay-nav` | How many videos in a row started on their own, for the "still watching?" check. Cleared when you close the tab. |

You can clear these in your browser settings. The site still works afterwards; you will need to sign in again and set your preferences again.

## Analytics and tracking

We do not use analytics or tracking cookies or scripts. We count views and watch time on our own servers as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## Ads

{{#ads}}
We show ads from Google AdSense on video pages, next to public videos. Google, and sometimes its partners, may use cookies or similar identifiers to show and measure ads. They do this under their own policies. See Google's page on how it uses information from sites that use its services.

{{#personalisedAds}}
Ads may be personalised using your activity across sites. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, ads are personalised only if you agree in the consent message you are shown. You can change your choice in your Google ad settings.
{{/personalisedAds}}
{{^personalisedAds}}
We ask Google for **non-personalised** ads. They depend on the page you are looking at and general information such as your approximate location, not on your browsing history or an advertising profile. Google may still use cookies or identifiers for measuring ads, preventing fraud and limiting how often you see an ad.
{{/personalisedAds}}

We do not show ads to the owner of a video on their own videos, on private or unlisted videos, in the player, or on sign-in, settings and Studio pages. We limit ads on videos that have been reported until a moderator has checked them. If you see an ad that breaks Google's policies, tell us from the [contact page](/contact).
{{/ads}}
{{^ads}}
We do not show ads at the moment. If we start, this page will say what changes before the first ad appears.
{{/ads}}
