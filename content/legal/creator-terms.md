## 1. Who can be a creator

You need an account with a confirmed email address, and you must be 18 or older. You accept these terms once before your first upload, and again when we change them in a way that matters.

## 2. What you promise about your videos

- You made the video, or you have permission from everyone whose work, voice, image or music is in it, and your permission covers publishing it here.
- It does not break the [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines), the [Copyright policy](/legal/copyright) or the law.
- Its title, thumbnail and description describe what the video is. They do not mislead viewers.
- If a brand paid you or gave you something of value for it, you ticked **includes paid promotion** so viewers see a label. Follow any law that applies to advertising and endorsements.
- You will not upload more than the limits we publish in [Uploading videos](/help/uploading-videos) allow.

## 3. Licence and removal

The licence you give us in the Terms covers the videos you upload. We may remove, limit or take ads off any video that breaks the rules. If we remove a video we tell you why where we can.

This is not tax advice.

## 8. Fraud and abuse

You must not tip yourself, buy or trade views, likes, comments, subscribers or tips, use bots or click farms, use stolen payment methods, or give false identity or payout details. If we find or reasonably suspect this, we may remove the views or tips involved, withhold or reverse money, hold payouts, remove videos, and suspend or close the account. Serious cases are reported to our payment provider and, where required, to the authorities. See also [Earning rules](/help/earning-rules).

## 9. No guarantee of income

Nothing here promises that you will earn any money. How much you receive depends on viewers choosing to send tips.

## 10. Ending

If your account ends, we pay out any available balance that is not under investigation or subject to a legal hold, once you ask and it meets the minimum, or earlier at our option. Money that cannot be paid because your details are wrong or you do not respond is handled as the law requires.
