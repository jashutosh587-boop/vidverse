## 1. What we collect and why

| What | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| **Account details:** name, handle, email address, a one-way scrambled form of your password, the date you confirmed you are over 18 and accepted the Terms | To create and secure your account and contact you about it |
| **Profile:** description, avatar colour, support links, settings such as autoplay and your daily reminder | To run your channel and the choices you set |
| **Your content:** videos (and the converted copies and thumbnails we make), titles, descriptions, tags, comments, replies | To provide the Service |
| **Activity while signed in:** videos watched and how far, likes and dislikes, subscriptions, saved videos, notifications | To resume videos, show your history, recommend videos and run the Service |
| **Counting views:** how long you watched, and a scrambled identifier that changes every day, made from your account if you are signed in or from your network address and browser details if you are not | To count views and watch time once, and to spot automated traffic, without keeping a record that follows you from day to day |
| **Security records:** sign-in times, browser and device description, a shortened network address, failed attempts, and the actions of moderators and admins | To protect accounts, investigate abuse and keep a record of important actions |
| **Messages and reports:** what you send through the contact form or the Report link | To reply and to act |
| **Two-step verification:** an encrypted secret and scrambled recovery codes | To protect your account if you turn it on |
{{#payments}}| **Payments:** tips you send or receive (amount, message, status, time) and, for creators, payout requests and payout details (UPI ID or bank account, held encrypted) | To pass on tips, pay creators, prevent fraud and keep the accounts the law requires |

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or the content of your device. We do not use your data for advertising profiles. {{#ads}}Ads are shown by Google, which handles its own data, as explained in [Cookies and ads](/legal/cookies-and-ads).{{/ads}}

## 2. How we use it

To provide the Service; to keep it safe and fair; to recommend videos (using what you watched, only to order what you see); to answer you; to meet the law; and to understand how the Service is used in totals. We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **Service providers** who run parts of the Service for us under contract: our hosting and storage provider, our email delivery provider{{#payments}}, our payment provider (Razorpay){{/payments}}{{#ads}}, and Google for ads{{/ads}}{{#aiAssistant}}, and our AI provider for the help assistant (your question and matching help text only){{/aiAssistant}}.
- **Other people, as part of the Service:** your public videos, comments and channel are visible to anyone. A tip's sender is shown to the creator unless the sender chose to be anonymous.
- **Authorities,** where the law, a court order or a valid legal request requires, or where needed to protect someone's safety.
- **A buyer or successor** if the Service is transferred, who must honour this policy.

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to run the Service. See [Cookies and ads](/legal/cookies-and-ads). We do not use analytics or tracking scripts.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account, profile, your videos and comments | Until you delete them or your account |
| Watch history, likes, saved videos | Until you remove them or delete your account |
| Sign-in sessions | At most 60 days, and 14 days after you were last active |
| Confirmation and reset links | They expire within 24 hours (one hour for password resets) and are deleted a week after that |
| Notifications | 90 days |
| Daily view and watch-time records | About 13 months |
| Messages you send us | As long as needed to deal with them and any follow-up |
| Records of actions by moderators and admins | As long as needed for accountability and legal claims |
| Payment, payout and accounting records | As long as the law requires us to keep them, even after you delete your account |

When you delete your account we remove or anonymise the rest, as described in [Deleting your account](/help/deleting-your-account). Copies in backups are removed as the backups are replaced.

## 6. How we protect it

Passwords are stored with a slow one-way function. Payout details and two-step secrets are encrypted. Sessions and security tokens are random and can be revoked. The Service is served over HTTPS with strict security headers. Access to personal data is limited to people who need it, and important actions are logged. No service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Depending on the law that applies to you, you can ask to: see the personal data we hold about you; get a copy; correct it; have it deleted; withdraw consent we relied on; and complain about how we handled it. You can do many of these yourself: download your data and delete your account in [Settings](/settings/privacy), edit your profile in [Settings](/settings/profile), and clear your watch history on the [History page](/history). For anything else, or if you want to nominate someone to exercise your rights for you, write to our [grievance officer](/legal/grievance). We will reply as quickly as we can and within the time the law requires. We may need to check that you are who you say you are.

## 8. Children

The Service is for people who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, and we do not track or target ads at children. If you think a child has an account, tell us through the [contact page](/contact) and we will close it and delete its data.

## 9. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data on servers in India or in other countries. We choose providers that protect it under contract, and we will not transfer it where the law does not allow.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we will tell you on the site and ask you to accept the new version where the law requires.
