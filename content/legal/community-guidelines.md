These guidelines apply from {{effectiveDate}} to everything on {{siteName}}: videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags, comments, channel names and profile text. They explain the Terms of Service. We apply them with judgement. Context matters, and so does the harm that content can do.

## Keep children safe

We do not allow sexual content involving anyone under 18, sexualised images of minors, grooming, or content that puts minors at risk. We remove it, close the account and report it to the authorities. Do not post anything that shows or invites the abuse of a child, even as a joke or for "awareness".

## Do not post sexual content

Pornography and sexually explicit material are not allowed, including in thumbnails and titles. Non-explicit nudity can be allowed for clearly educational, documentary or artistic reasons, with care about how it is titled and shown. Sharing intimate images of someone without their consent is never allowed.

## No violence, threats or dangerous acts

Do not threaten people, call for violence, or praise violent groups or attacks. Do not show or promote dangerous challenges or ways to hurt yourself or others. Real footage of serious violence is allowed only when it is news or education and handled responsibly. Content that encourages self-harm or eating disorders is not allowed. If you are struggling, speak to someone you trust or a local helpline.

## No harassment or hate

Do not harass, bully, stalk or target people, share their private information (doxxing) or encourage others to do so. Do not attack people for their race, caste, religion, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or nationality. Criticising ideas, public figures' public actions, or institutions is fine.

## No scams, spam or fake engagement

No scams, fake giveaways, phishing, links to malware, or deceptive money-making offers. No repeated or irrelevant posts. No buying, selling or trading of views, likes, comments, subscribers or tips. No misleading titles or thumbnails: they must describe the video.

## No impersonation or deception

Do not pretend to be another person, channel or organisation. Do not present made-up or altered media as real in a way that could mislead people or harm them. If you use artificially generated or altered content that looks real, say so.

## Respect other people's rights

Post only what you have the right to post. See the [Copyright policy](/legal/copyright). Do not use others' names, images, voices or personal data without permission in ways that harm them.

## Follow the law

Nothing that is illegal in India may be posted, including content that promotes illegal drugs, weapons trafficking, gambling that is not lawful, or other crimes.

## Label paid promotion

If a brand paid you or gave you something for a video, tick **includes paid promotion** so viewers see a label.

## Context, news and art

News, education, documentary, satire and art may deal with hard subjects. Explain what you are showing and why. We may add a warning, limit who finds it, or take ads off it even where we leave it up.

## What happens when rules are broken

We may remove content, limit it, take ads off it, suspend or close an account, hold payouts, and report to the authorities where the law requires. We aim to say what we did and why, and to let you ask for a second look from the [contact page](/contact). Serious breaks can lead to immediate removal.

## Reporting

Use **Report** next to the video or comment, or the [contact page](/contact). See [Reporting content](/help/reporting-content).
